Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

TL;DR: SafeCatalog collects no personal data, uses no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no backend server. Everything runs locally in your browser.

1. Overview

SafeCatalog ("we", "us", "our") is a fully static website hosted on GitHub Pages. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect (spoiler: almost none), how we use it, and your rights.

Because SafeCatalog has no backend, no database, and no server-side code, our ability to collect personal data is structurally limited to what the browser itself exposes.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

SafeCatalog does not have any forms, accounts, or user-submitted content. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

The "Report an issue" button opens your own email client via a mailto: link. Any email you choose to send goes directly to our address — we do not intercept or log it through any web service.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

GitHub Pages (our hosting provider, operated by GitHub, Inc., a subsidiary of Microsoft) may collect standard server access logs when you visit this site. This may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Referring URL
  • Date and time of access

This data is collected by GitHub, not by us, and is subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

2.3 Local Storage

We store one item in your browser's localStorage: your dark/light theme preference (value: "dark" or "light"). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

3. Cookies

We do not set any cookies. None. Zero. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or functional cookies beyond what your browser may set automatically.

4. Third-Party Services

SafeCatalog loads the following third-party resources:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — loads JetBrains Mono and DM Sans typefaces. Google may log your IP address as part of this request. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com) — loads the CSS framework. The CDN provider may log standard access data.
  • External download links — clicking "Download / Link" takes you to third-party sites (GitHub, official project pages, etc.). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

We have no control over data collected by these third parties.

5. Analytics

We do not use Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, Cloudflare Web Analytics, or any other analytics service. We have no visibility into how many people visit the site or what they click.

6. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data because we do not collect any. Your theme preference stored in localStorage is retained until you clear your browser data.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, and portability of your data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete. To remove your theme preference, clear your browser's local storage for this site.

8. Children's Privacy

SafeCatalog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@safecatalog.one.
